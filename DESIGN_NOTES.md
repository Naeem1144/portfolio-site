# Portfolio design direction

## What felt impersonal

The previous page had a consistent palette and typography, but its hierarchy read like a resume. The long opening competed with availability, qualifications, contact details and a technical diagram. Eight equally weighted project rows made the scroll repetitive. The personal story arrived after that long list.

## The organising idea

A marketing mind working with data. Curiosity is the thread connecting the opening, the featured customer study and the personal story. The user selected near-black and acid yellow. Use a graphite canvas, raised charcoal panels, silver supporting text and acid-yellow actions. A custom NN monogram is drawn as a connected signal with a highlighted sample point. This repeats in the header, favicon, touch icon, social card and footer. Geist replaces serif italics throughout.

## Changes

- Shorter opening with a personal introduction and one primary action.
- A finished, paused model as the initial visual. Visitors can replay the learning sequence, inspect raw points and pause motion. The surface carries an error axis and both minima are labelled with their loss, so the claim that only one of them is the lowest can be checked. Reduced motion resolves the sequence immediately.
- One featured hotel study, paired supporting projects and a full-width final experiment.
- Existing project numbers, source notes, code links and filters preserved.
- Personal background expressed through a more specific heading.
- Full skills evidence available in a keyboard-accessible disclosure.
- A direct contact section and large name sign-off with the signal monogram.
- Responsive layout, focus states, section tracking, reduced-motion support retained. The chosen dark identity stays consistent across system color preferences.

## Keep the direction coherent

Prioritise a few meaningful stories over adding more badges or decorative motion. New projects should explain the question, the work and its limits. Keep illustrative diagrams labelled as illustrations. A real portrait or authentic project screenshots could add personal texture later; avoid stock imagery or fabricated results.

## Verification

The preceding layout pass verified build, TypeScript, ESLint, project filters, disclosures, animation controls, reduced motion and empty contact-form validation. The visual identity pass additionally checks the chosen palette, contrast, responsive typography and brand assets. No email is sent during UI verification.
